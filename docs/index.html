Living PRD

Product Requirements Document

Updated every week as we test with real users and learn what works.

1. Target User & The Job to Be Done

Who is this for? Me, as the person who usually organizes group trips with friends. I'll reach testers through our group chat.

The painful "Before" workflow: Someone proposes a trip in the group chat. The organizer researches stays, restaurants, and activities across Airbnb, Google, TikTok, and blogs, then dumps links in the chat, where most get buried or ignored. People reply slowly or vaguely ("I'm down for anything"), so the organizer guesses what people want, builds the plan alone, and chases everyone about money and bookings. One person does all the work, decisions stall, and the final plan rarely reflects what everyone wanted.

The "After" workflow with this product: The organizer enters destination, dates, group size, budget, and vibe, and pastes links to a few Airbnb or VRBO options the group is considering. Each friend opens the trip link and fills out a quick profile — budget comfort, food preferences and restrictions, activity interests, energy level, and dealbreakers — and Fernweh combines everyone's profiles to understand the group. Fernweh suggests restaurants and activities that fit the whole group, so nobody has to research or paste links for those (friends can optionally add a spot they already know about). Everyone swipes right or left on restaurants and activities; stays get a comparison view with key details, links, and cost per person. Fernweh shows group matches, builds 2–3 itinerary options from them (for example, relaxed, packed, and budget-friendly), each with cost per person, the group votes on a final plan, and Fernweh creates a booking plan with what to book and when.

2. Scope & Explicit Non-Goals

  • In Scope (Core Workflow): Trip setup, per-friend profiles via the trip link, AI-suggested restaurant and activity options, group swiping, and a results view with matches.
  • Out of Scope #1: Booking or paying for anything inside the app.
  • Out of Scope #2: Direct connections to Airbnb, VRBO, OpenTable, or Resy — stays are added as pasted links.
  • Out of Scope #3: User accounts and profiles saved across trips — each friend fills out a quick profile per trip through the shared link.
  • Next Up: Structured friend profiles, hotel suggestions, a group decision view, and veto tokens are next for Build 3 — see the full roadmap for what's planned through Build 5.

3. How It Works Under the Hood & Riskiest Assumption (Week 2+)

Riskiest Assumption: AI can suggest real, relevant places that fit a whole group's mixed preferences.

How Build 2 tests it: I ran a real 2-person Lisbon trip and checked all 16 suggestions on Google Maps. 16 of 16 were real, open, and a good fit. Caveat: one small trip to a well-known city; next I'll test larger groups with more conflicting preferences, less famous destinations, and pod testers' own trips. Thumbs up/down votes will show whether suggestions fit real people.

How it works: Browser form (frontend, static/index.html) → local Python server (backend, app.py, reads the OpenRouter key from .env) → AI model via OpenRouter → JSON back to the browser, shown as cards. The key never reaches the browser.
