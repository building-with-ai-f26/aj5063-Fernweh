# Fernweh Roadmap

Positioning: Other apps help you organize a trip. Fernweh helps your group agree on one, and gets smarter about your group's taste with every trip.

## Build 3 (due Nov 23): The group decides

**Must-have:**
- Structured friend profiles (budget comfort, dietary needs, interests, energy level, dealbreakers, optional notes)
- Avoid duplicate suggestions
- Hotel suggestions (same cards and voting as restaurants/activities)
- Group results view: "Everyone's in," "Split decision," "Pass"
- Veto tokens (1-2 per person)

**Stretch:**
- Compromise finder (AI resolves split decisions)
- Budget fairness check
- "Swap this card" button

## Build 4 (due Nov 30): From matches to a plan

**Must-have:**
- Real place data and photos via a places API (actual photos, ratings, hours; replaces AI-only suggestions)
- Airbnb comparison table (paste links, compare, vote, cost per person)
- Auto-built itinerary: 2-3 versions from group matches

**Stretch:**
- Packing list and reminders
- Destination FAQ and transit tips, with "verify this" labels

Plus: testing track with real users

## Build 5 (due Dec 7): Live for the whole group

**Must-have:**
- Cloud launch: join by link, no download
- Add a friend mid-planning, with a catch-up view
- Anonymous voting
- Decision deadlines, with a "who hasn't voted" view

**Stretch:**
- Shared final schedule with links
- Light bill splitting (cost per person + who-paid-what log)
- Email/text nudges

## Future vision

- Taste profile that learns from in-trip feedback
- Friends' favorites by location
- Destination comparison voting
- Partner perks and booking commissions (inspired by Fora)
